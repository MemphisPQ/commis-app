Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

The short version

  • We never sell anyone's data, to anyone, and we aren't here to collect it.
  • Your recipes, prices and counts are there to run Commis for you. That's all.
  • Your invoice PDFs are read in your browser. They only leave it if you choose to send them to us, and we delete our copy once the reader is built.
  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no cookies of ours.

This policy explains what information Commis (commisapp.ca) handles, why, and what your rights are. Commis is run by Sweets Suite, a business based in Quebec, Canada.

1. What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, your restaurant's name, and the date and version of the terms you accepted. Your password is stored encrypted: we can't read it.
  • What you enter: items, suppliers, prices and their history, recipes, menus, counts, allergens, notes and settings.
  • Invoices you send us, only if you press the button to do so: the PDF, its name and your note, if any. An invoice can contain prices and your restaurant's and your supplier's contact details.
  • Error reports: if Commis runs into an error in your browser, it sends us a short anonymous report: the kind of error and where in our code it happened, the page, your browser and system type (for example "Safari 17 on iOS 17.5"), your screen width and the page's language. It isn't linked to your account, and it contains nothing you've entered: no email address, restaurant name, recipe, item, price or invoice. We don't store your IP address with it.

2. What we don't receive

  • Invoices you don't send us. A PDF added on the Invoices page is read in your browser and doesn't leave it. Only what you save goes to your account, like anything else you enter: its supplier, date, lines and amounts.
  • Your card number. If you buy us a soda, that happens on our page at Ko-fi, and PayPal or Stripe takes the payment. We see the amount, the date and what you give there, such as your name, your email and a message, never the card number.

3. Why we use it

Only to run the Service, protect it and build it: signing you in, showing and calculating your data, sending emails about your account (confirmation, new password, an important change), finding and fixing errors, and teaching Commis to read new kinds of invoices. We send no advertising and don't use your data for advertising. We don't look at your recipes or prices, unless you ask us to, for example to fix a problem, or the law requires it.

4. Who handles it for us

We don't sell or rent your information. Only these companies handle it, to run the Service. They may do so outside Quebec, including in the United States, where other laws apply:

  • Supabase: accounts, data, invoices you send and error reports;
  • Netlify: hosting the site (IP address and technical details of visits, in its logs);
  • Resend: sending account emails (your email address), and the email that tells us an invoice you sent has arrived (its file name and your note, never the PDF);
  • Google (Gmail): our inbox, which receives that email and anything you write to us;
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare): the lettering and the PDF-reading tools download from them, which shows them your IP address.

If you buy us a soda, you leave Commis for our page at Ko-fi. Ko-fi and the payment company (PayPal or Stripe) handle what you enter there, under their own privacy policies.

We could also have to disclose information where the law requires it.

5. What stays in your browser

Commis uses no cookies and no statistics or tracking tools. It keeps in your browser's local storage your sign-in token, your language, your filters and display choices, your drafts, invoices in progress, and what you've put in the trash. You can clear them in your browser's settings; you will then have to sign in again, and anything not yet saved will be lost.

6. How long

  • Your account and data: as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete it, they are deleted within 30 days.
  • An invoice you send us: once the reader is built, or if we find we can't build one, we delete the file and your note, along with the email that told us it arrived. The reader itself holds how to read that layout, not your invoice or your prices.
  • Error reports: deleted automatically after 60 days.
  • Tips: we, Ko-fi and the payment company keep what tax and accounting laws require.
  • A hosting company's routine backups and logs may take a little while to clear.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see your information, correct it or have it deleted, withdraw your consent, and find out how it's handled. Settings also lets you download a full copy of your data, in a common format. Write to the address below; we reply within 30 days. If you aren't satisfied with our reply, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Security

Connections to the site are encrypted. In the database, rules limit each row to the account that created it: one restaurant can't read or change another's data. No system is perfect. If an incident could cause you serious harm, we would tell you and the Commission d'accès à l'information, as the law requires.

9. Google Drive

Commis doesn't connect to Google Drive yet. When that option is offered, it will be optional and work like this:

  • Commis will only ask for access to the files and folders you pick in Google's own picker, and those it creates for you (drive.file). It won't see the rest of your Drive.
  • It will use them only for what you ask: reading the invoices and templates you pick, and saving your cards, sheets and backups to your Drive.
  • Those files are handled in your browser, between you and Google. Google's permission stays in your browser's memory and expires after about an hour. In your account we keep only the name and ID of the files and folders you picked, to find them again.
  • We don't sell this data, share it with anyone or use it for advertising. Nobody here reads it, unless you send us a file yourself or the law requires it.
  • Commis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You will be able to remove that permission at any time in your Google account.

10. Children

The Service is meant for restaurants and other food businesses, not for children.

11. Changes

The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects your rights, we will email you before it takes effect, as our Terms of Use set out.
